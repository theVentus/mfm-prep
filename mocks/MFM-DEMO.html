General Instructions

  1. Total duration of the examination is minutes. The clock is set at the server; the countdown timer at the top right shows the time remaining. When the timer reaches zero, the examination ends by itself. You need not submit your paper.
  2. The paper has questions in the following sections. You may move freely between sections and questions within the total time.
  3. Marking scheme: +1 for every correct answer, −0.25 for every incorrect answer, 0 for a question not answered.
  4. The Question Palette on the right shows the status of each question using the symbols below:
    1 You have not visited the question yet.
    2 You have not answered the question.
    3 You have answered the question.
    4 You have NOT answered the question, but have marked it for review.
    5 You have answered the question and marked it for review. This answer will be considered for evaluation.
  5. To answer: click an option, then click SAVE & NEXT. If you move to another question without saving, your choice is not saved.
  6. SAVE & MARK FOR REVIEW saves your answer and marks it for review. MARK FOR REVIEW & NEXT marks the question for review without saving a new choice. CLEAR removes your saved answer.
  7. Your responses are saved continuously. If the system closes unexpectedly, reopen the test: you will resume where you left off, and the timer will have kept running.
  8. Camera and microphone stay on throughout. Exiting full screen or switching tabs/windows is recorded with time.
